Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Welcome to Watermark Camera (Photo Frame EXIF). Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains how our application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Images and Media: Our app requires access to your camera and storage to process photos. Important: We do not collect, store, or transmit your photos to any external servers. All image processing (adding frames, EXIF reading) happens locally on your device.

Device Information: We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as device model, OS version, and unique device identifiers for crash reporting and analytics to improve app performance.

2. Permissions Used

  • CAMERA: To take new photos directly within the app.
  • READ/WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE: To select photos from your gallery and save the framed images back to your device.
  • INTERNET: Required for displaying advertisements and collecting anonymous analytics.

3. Offline Processing

The core functionality of Photo Frame EXIF is designed to work offline. Your privacy is guaranteed as your personal media never leaves your phone during the framing or collage process.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: For app functionality.
  • AdMob: To show advertisements. AdMob may use cookies or similar technologies to serve personalized ads.
  • Firebase Analytics: To understand how users interact with the app and to improve user experience.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
